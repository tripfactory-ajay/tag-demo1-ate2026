Travel Credits

Everything you need to know about earning, using, and managing your travel credits

What Are Travel Credits?

  • Travel Credits are rewards you earn for every booking you make with us.
  • They act as a benefit that helps you save on your future trips.
  • Credits accumulate as you continue booking and travelling with us.

How to Earn Travel Credits?

  • Earn Travel Credits every time you make an eligible booking.
  • The number of credits you earn may vary by product, journey, or promotion.
  • The expected Travel Credits will be clearly shown while confirming your booking.

How to Redeem Travel Credits?

  • Travel Credits become available for redemption after your trip is completed.
  • You can redeem your credits on your next booking.
  • Credits can be used on every booking you make, helping you save on future travel.
